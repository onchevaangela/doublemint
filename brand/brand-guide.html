Brand guidelines for Doublemint Detailing. The new identity drops the busy cartoon badge for one simple mark that reads at any size, on a business card, a shirt, a van door or an Instagram avatar.

01 · The mark

Built for speed

The mark is a low sports coupe with speed lines behind it, so anyone can see at a glance that this is car work. The mint leaf on the door is the team decal, and the lime hubs match it.

The name is set in a heavy, forward-leaning italic over a racing stripe, the look of motorsport livery and car-show banners.

It is still flat: no gradients, glow or bevels. That way it prints in one color, embroiders cleanly and can be cut from vinyl for the side of a truck.

03 · Color

Black, chrome and mint

Mostly Gloss Black with Spearmint as the accent. That keeps the look you already have on the flyer, just calmer. Aim for about 70% black, 20% neutrals and 10% green.

04 · Typography

Heavy italic, clean body

DETAILING
Inter Display Black Italic · headlines, prices, logo
Professional Services

Inter Regular / Medium for body text, service lists and contact details. It is free on Google Fonts and works in Canva.

Inter SemiBold, +0.4em tracking · labels, taglines

Stick to these two styles. Leave out script fonts, 3D text, chrome effects and outlines.

05 · Voice

Confident, clean, friendly

Fresh look.

Keep it short and plain. Use real prices and real photos of your own work. Leave out the hype words (“ultimate”, “insane”) and use one exclamation mark at most.

06 · Do & don’t

Keep the logo intact

Do

  • Use the files in logo/ exactly as supplied
  • Use the white version over photos
  • Put the logo on Gloss Black, Foam or Evergreen
  • Scale it proportionally

Don’t

  • Add glow, gradients, shadows, bevels or outlines
  • Add bubbles, sprayers, sparkles or a second car
  • Stretch it, flip the car or recolor the decal
  • Put the color logo on a busy photo
